Hours worked: ~3-4h

First real deep case study on the site — MANTHIRA. This one came with a wrinkle none of the placeholder pages had: the project is under NDA, and the site's repo is public. Most of the session was about solving that constraint rather than working around it — building a page that reads as a proper case study without ever showing a single frame of the actual storyboards.

NDA-safe page strategy

  • Real storyboard/previz/thumbnail images stay out of the tracked site folders entirely — the only real MANTHIRA assets committed are the title card image and the title animation video, both explicitly cleared for public use
  • The rest of the visual interest comes from synthetic placeholder images (generated, clearly labeled) standing in for the thumbnail/storyboard/previz/rigging stages until real stills are ready to swap in
  • Added a real "Request Access" section at the bottom — a proper gated form, not a mailto link, reusing the homepage's Web3Forms contact-form pattern (same fields, same JS handlers, just re-scoped to a MANTHIRA subject line)

A page-specific "magical" theme

  • New scoped theme, .case-noir — gold / teal / magic / ruby accent tokens pulled from the real title card and character art, not arbitrary picks
  • Still fully respects the site's light/dark toggle — each token gets its own light and dark value rather than being hardcoded to one look
  • Title treatment uses Pirata One (blackletter) with a blurred gradient glow behind the gradient-text fill, echoing the title card's own lettering
