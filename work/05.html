MANTHIRA — Title Animation

A standalone CGI title sequence, built as part of the wider MANTHIRA project.

Alongside storyboarding and previz for MANTHIRA (see the full case study), I also built out the film's CGI title animation — the metallic gold lettering, the glow, the reveal. It's a different kind of work from boards: less about staging a scene, more about giving a title its own presence before the story even starts.
